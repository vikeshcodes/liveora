# Code Of Conduct

Vikesh Codes Creator OS is a community-friendly project. We want contributors to feel welcome, respected, and able to learn.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Help beginners when you can.
- Give feedback on code, not people.
- Assume good intent.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment or discrimination
- Personal attacks
- Trolling or insulting comments
- Publishing private information
- Sharing secrets, tokens, or credentials
- Misleading use of VikeshCodes branding

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate these standards.

Repeated or severe violations may result in being blocked from participating in the project.
